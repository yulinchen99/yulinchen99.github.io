I am **Yulin Chen**, a PhD student at New York University, co-advised by [Prof. Chen Zhao](http://www.chenz.umiacs.io/) and [Prof. He He](https://hhexiy.github.io/). I am generally interested in understanding language model behaviors, particularly on *reasoning* and *representation engineering/alignment*. I obtained bachelor and master degrees both from Tsinghua University. During my master, I have worked on prompting and large language model alignment at [THUNLP](https://nlp.csai.tsinghua.edu.cn/).
